About LeedsPHP

LeedsPHP is an established non-profit user group set up as a community to share knowledge about PHP, Software Engineering, DevOps, Cloud Computing, Testing, Architecture, Leadership, Careers, Soft Skills.

We create a welcoming and diverse environment and we welcome speakers of any skill level and experience.

We meet on the third Wednesday of the month (usually) for nine meetups each year, with social events in July and December.
